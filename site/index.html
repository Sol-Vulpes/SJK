What SJK brings

SJK is Sol's vision of Jedi Academy, developed by Sol with contributors from the community.

The SJK UI

  • The ringThe main page: SJK's emblem in a turning ring, the menu beside it, recent servers and SJK chat, over a camera tour of Yavin Training Grounds.
  • SettingsSimple and Advanced views, a detail column saying what each setting does, and a search that finds any of them.
  • Character creatorOne grid of tiles for model, head, torso, legs, skin, hat and cape. Every hat and cape is open to everyone.
  • ServersA sortable list from two master servers, names in their colours, favourites, and the map's levelshot and players beside it.
  • Loading to scoreboardThe same look from the first connection to the last round, with the classic versions one setting away.

Classic Jedi Academy

  • Classic menusMain, profile, setup, controls and the join-server browser laid out like retail, one switch away in First setup or Settings.
  • Classic scoreboardClient IDs, compact rows on full servers, and JoF EternalJK's rules for when it opens.
  • Game HUDs from your filesThe original HUD, or any custom HUD pack that replaces ui/hud.menu.
  • Sharp typeThe game's fonts redrawn as vector fonts, crisp at 4K, with Russian, Japanese and emoji in chat and names.

Collection

  • HolocronsFound as you play, and opened crate-style for saber shaders and cards.
  • Saber shadersBlades from Common to Mythical, Creyon's 128 Blade Lab signature blades among them, each with its own sounds.
  • Blade cardsA card for every shader holocrons drop; holding one makes crafting its shader cheaper.
  • Medals and achievementsShown on your profile, your card and the scoreboard.

Toys and emotes

  • Ragdolltoy_ragdoll makes you go limp and fall; every SJK player sees the same fall, and lifts and trains carry you.
  • Dronefreecam and peek fly a training remote others see. Nobody is peeked without their consent.
  • Hax, Order 66, cardboard boxHack a willing player, die together when the order comes, or hide in a carton, Metal Gear style.
  • IlluminateA holocron floats by your shoulder and lights the way in dark maps.
  • Emotesemotes opens a picker; other SJK players on your server see them and hear their music.

Community servers

  • JA+ serversSJK joins as a JA+ plugin client, with RGB sabers, predicted JA+ moves without snap-backs, and JA+ tools under japlus.
  • JoF serversJoF tools under jof.: Destruction on the Force wheel, server dialogue, and JoF's own content when you opt in.
  • Siege and CTFRespawn waves, objectives and their announcements, the hack bar, and flag carriers named on the scoreboard.
  • Reliable joiningLost handshake packets and gamestates are recovered, and old 72-bone player models load.

Identity and chat

  • SJK identityA key made on your PC proves who you are to the SJK hub, with no account or password.
  • Profile and cardYour picture, bio, medals and blade, on a card other players can open.
  • SJK chatTalk with SJK players wherever they play; news from the SJK team shows there too.
  • Encrypted whispersWhispers between SJK players are encrypted end to end, with safety codes to compare.
  • Discord statusYour Discord profile shows the server, map and mode you play.

Graphics and comfort

  • Graphics presetsVanilla style, SJK (recommended) or Experimental real-time lighting, from one dropdown.
  • Modern lightingHDR, bloom, shadows, volumetrics, day and night, rain and snow, each in Settings › Graphics.
  • NameplatesHealth, shield and Force over players, shared between the SJK players who share theirs.
  • Quick wheelPages of actions, toys and emotes on one key, with a searchable catalogue.
  • Smooth pacingThe FPS cap holds its exact rate and can follow your monitor.

Why SJK

SJK aims to be a true JKA+: everything players expect from today's community clients, from the retail look and feel to mod servers such as JA+ and JoF and modern screens, in a client that stays clean and fast. No bloat: every feature has to earn its place and its frame time, and the original game's behaviour comes first.

Install

You need a legally owned copy of Star Wars Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy (Steam, GOG or disc). SJK ships no game files.

Windows 10/11, x64

  1. Extract into GameData

    Download the Windows ZIP and extract all of it into the game's GameData folder, beside base:

    GameData/
├─ base/          (assets0-3.pk3)
├─ sjk.exe         ← start
├─ sjk-server.exe
└─ README-SJK.txt

    On Steam: right-click Jedi Academy › Manage › Browse local files › GameData.

  2. Launch

    Start sjk. No path or setting is needed. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app because the builds are not code-signed; choose More info › Run anyway if you trust the download (checksums are in the release).

    Kept outside GameData, SJK finds a Steam copy of the game in any Steam library, and a GOG copy. To start SJK with Steam's Play button: right-click Jedi Academy › Properties › General › Launch options, and write the path of sjk.exe followed by %command%, for example "C:\Games\SJK\sjk.exe" %command%.

Linux, x64

  1. Extract into GameData

    Download the Linux ZIP and extract all of it into the game's GameData folder, beside base.

  2. Launch

    Run ./sjk. It needs a working Vulkan or OpenGL graphics driver.

Mac with Apple Silicon (experimental)

  1. Extract

    For M1 or later; there is no build for Intel Macs. Extract the Mac ZIP into the game's GameData folder, beside base; SJK also looks for the game in Steam's Mac library.

  2. Launch

    Open SJK.app. It is not signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it until you allow it once: its README says how. It has not been played on a Mac yet, so reports are welcome.

  1. Settings and updates

    Settings, binds, screenshots and demos live in GameData/SJK/. SJK checks for a new release when it starts and installs it from its Update page, keeping that folder. It opens on the SJK UI; First setup or Settings › Interface › Menu style switches to the classic menus.

  2. On JA+ servers

    SJK identifies as a JA+ plugin client. JA+ puts new players in its alternate dimension, where the grapple is off: use /amAltDim to leave it. serverconfig lists the server's JA+ options.

  3. From source

    Install Rust 1.88+, then cargo build --release -p sjk-viewer -p sjk-dedicated, which builds sjk and sjk-server. See the README for platform notes and the dedicated server.
