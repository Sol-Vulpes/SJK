Star Wars Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy multiplayer

Sol JK

A native Rust client and server for Jedi Academy, classic at heart: the retail menus, scoreboard and HUDs are back, JA+ servers feel right, and the game stays sharp on today's screens.

Download Source code

Windows 10/11 x64 and Linux x64 · requires your own copy of Jedi Academy

What SJK brings

SJK is Sol's vision of Jedi Academy, developed by Sol.

Classic Jedi Academy

  • Classic menus by default: main, profile, setup, controls and the join-server browser laid out like retail, with the SJK emblem glowing in the turning ring and glowing highlights.
  • Classic scoreboard with client IDs, compact rows on full servers and rows that glide when the order changes.
  • Game HUDs from your files: the original HUD, or any custom HUD pack that replaces ui/hud.menu.
  • Retail fonts on every surface the original drew with them, the ¬ logo glyph and the ^8/^9 colours.

Community servers

  • JA+ support: SJK joins as a JA+ plugin client, so RGB sabers and plugin features work.
  • Predicted JA+ moves: flip kick, yellow DFA, faster grip, g_debugMelee kicks and wall holds, the grapple hook and duel pass-through, without snap-backs.
  • serverconfig and pluginDisable console commands, as JA+ players know them.
  • Reliable joining: lost handshake packets and gamestates are recovered, and old 72-bone player models load.

Comfort and clarity

  • Renderer settings page for HDR, bloom, shadows, volumetrics and day/night, in Setup.
  • Sharp at 4K: HD levelshots keep their resolution and text scales with the window.
  • Safer controls: MOUSE1, MOUSE2 and ESC can't be rebound by accident, key names in capitals, keyboard-layout aware binds.
  • Smooth pacing: the FPS cap follows your monitor and holds its exact rate.

Why SJK

SJK aims to be a true JKA+: everything players expect from today's community clients, from the retail look and feel to mod servers such as JA+ and modern screens, in a client that stays clean and fast. No bloat: every feature has to earn its place and its frame time, and the original game's behaviour comes first.

Once the client is solid, a JoF edition of SJK will follow for the JoF community.

Install

  1. You need

    A legally owned copy of Star Wars Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy (Steam, GOG or disc) and Windows 10/11 x64 or Linux x64 with a working graphics driver. SJK ships no game assets.

  2. Extract into GameData

    Download the ZIP for your platform and extract all of it into the game's GameData folder, beside base:

    GameData/
├─ base/          (assets0-3.pk3)
├─ sjk.exe         ← start
├─ sjk-server.exe
└─ README-SJK.txt

    On Steam: right-click Jedi Academy › Manage › Browse local files › GameData.

  3. Launch

    Start sjk. No path or setting is needed. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app because the builds are not code-signed; choose More info › Run anyway if you trust the download (checksums are in the release).

  4. Your settings

    Settings, binds, screenshots and demos live in GameData/SJK/. SJK opens on the SJK UI; First setup or Settings › Interface › Menu style switches to the classic menus. To update, replace the executables and keep the SJK folder.

  5. On JA+ servers

    SJK identifies as a JA+ plugin client. JA+ puts new players in its alternate dimension, where the grapple is off: use /amAltDim to leave it. serverconfig lists the server's JA+ options.

  6. From source

    Install Rust 1.88+, then cargo build --release -p sjk-viewer -p sjk-dedicated, which builds sjk and sjk-server. See the README for platform notes and the dedicated server.
